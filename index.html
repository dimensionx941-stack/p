Chapter I · The Dedication

A Letter for Your Eighteenth

Dearest Paromita, eighteen is one of life’s rarest thresholds — a golden turning of the page where everything you have been gathers quietly into everything you are becoming. Today is not merely a number. It is a celebration of your heart, your grace, and the gentle wonder you bring into every room you enter.

You have grown into someone unforgettable: soft without ever being fragile, thoughtful without losing your spark, radiant in the most effortless way. Some beauty fades with seasons — yours is the kind that lives in your laughter, your kindness, and the sincerity you carry everywhere.

So take your time with the mornings you love. Chase the dreams that quicken your pulse. Guard your peace, keep your wonder, and never doubt how far your wings can carry you. The world is vast, Paromita — and it is brighter with you stepping into it.

With endless admiration

Always cheering for you

Chapter IV · Verse for Her

Eighteen Petals of Light

Eighteen springs have gently taught the sky
the softness of a morning brushed in rose;
a quiet grace the hurried world walks by,
yet lingers everywhere her laughter goes.

May every door before her open wide,
may silver moons watch kindly as she dreams;
hold fast the tender flame she keeps inside —
she is the poetry of turning eighteen.
